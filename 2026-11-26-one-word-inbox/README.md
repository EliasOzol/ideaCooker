# OneWord Inbox

> Catch the thought in one word now, so you don't remember it on the way home.

**Daily idea · 2026-11-26**

## The idea

A very simple place to drop a thought the moment it shows up while you switch between tasks.
Capture should take seconds, either as a short voice recording or as a quick note.

The note can be cryptic. One or two words that only you understand are enough to recall the task later, for example `STEEN BESKED`.

The captured items go into two lists:

- **Need to do:** things you have to finish
- **Nice to know:** things worth remembering, but not urgent

## Why

- Working on several things at once means small tasks get lost between switches.
- Remembering at home that you needed to do X and Y is too late to act on it.
- Writing a full note breaks focus. A single word keeps the interruption short.

## The prototype

No prototype yet. A first version could be a single page with one text field and a record button, where each entry is sorted into "Need to do" or "Nice to know" and can be ticked off.
