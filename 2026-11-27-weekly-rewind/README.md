# Weekly Rewind

> One look back at the week: what got made, and what slipped through the cracks.

**Daily idea · 2026-11-27**

## The idea

Weekly Rewind is a weekly recap that gives an overview of the past week:

- what has been made
- what was started but maybe forgotten

## Why

- It is easy to lose track of what you actually did in a week.
- Things that were started and then dropped are easy to forget without a reminder.

## The prototype

Not built yet. A first prototype could be a small script or page that lists the week's activity in one place and flags the items that haven't been touched since.
