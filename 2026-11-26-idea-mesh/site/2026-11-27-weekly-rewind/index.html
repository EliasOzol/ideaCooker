Weekly Rewind

A weekly look back at what I made and what I started but may have forgotten. Still an idea.

Weekly Rewind is an idea for a weekly recap. It shows what got made over the past week, and it also points out the things I started and then quietly dropped.

I wanted it because I easily lose track of what I actually did in a week. Half-finished things are the easiest to forget. Without a reminder they just slip away.

Nothing is built yet. My first prototype will probably be a small script or page that lists the week's activity in one place and flags anything I haven't touched since. Before building it, I need to decide what the recap covers, where its data comes from, and when and how it gets delivered.
